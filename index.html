一、我们不收集任何个人数据

K线之神是一款完全离线运行的应用。我们不会收集、存储、传输或分享您的任何个人信息，包括但不限于：

  • 姓名、邮箱、电话号码等身份信息
  • 设备标识符、IP 地址、位置信息
  • 使用行为、操作习惯等分析数据
  • 通讯录、照片、文件等设备内容

应用内不存在任何第三方统计、广告或跟踪 SDK。

二、数据存储

您的游戏记录（答题成绩、命中率、盘感积分、设置偏好等）仅保存在您自己的设备上，不会上传到任何服务器。卸载应用后，这些数据将被系统删除。

三、订阅与支付

订阅购买与支付全程由 Apple App Store 处理。我们只能看到由 Apple 提供的匿名订阅状态（有效 / 已过期），无法接触您的 Apple ID、银行卡或其他支付信息。退订路径：iPhone「设置」→ 您的 Apple 账户 →「订阅」。

四、网络访问

除 App Store 的订阅验证（由系统 StoreKit 框架发起）外，本应用不主动访问网络。

五、儿童隐私

本应用面向 4 岁以上所有年龄段。由于我们不收集任何数据，不存在儿童个人信息被收集的情形。

六、政策变更

如本政策发生变更，我们将在本页面更新并注明生效日期。重大变更时，我们会在应用内以显著方式提示。

七、联系我们

如有任何隐私相关问题，请联系：tang-yu-bin@qq.com

ChartKensei · Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026  ·  Applies to: ChartKensei (K线之神)

中文版 ↑

1. We Collect No Personal Data

ChartKensei is a fully offline application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, including but not limited to:

  • Identity information such as name, email, or phone number
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data
  • Usage or behavioral analytics
  • Contacts, photos, files, or other on-device content

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

2. Data Storage

Your game records (scores, accuracy, instinct points, preferences) are stored only on your own device and are never uploaded to any server. Deleting the app removes this data.

3. Subscription & Payment

All purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. We only receive an anonymous subscription status (active / expired) from Apple and never have access to your Apple ID, bank card, or payment details. To cancel: iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

4. Network Access

The app does not access the network except for App Store subscription verification performed by the system StoreKit framework.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is rated 4+. Since we collect no data at all, no children's personal information is ever collected.

6. Changes to This Policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will be prominently communicated in the app.

7. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact: tang-yu-bin@qq.com
