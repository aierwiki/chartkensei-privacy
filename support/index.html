常见问题 / FAQ

如何恢复购买？/ How do I restore purchases?

中文：打开 App，在付费墙页面点击「恢复购买」即可在同一 Apple ID 下恢复订阅。

English: Open the app and tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall to restore your subscription with the same Apple ID.

免费试用如何运作？/ How does the free trial work?

中文：新用户可获得 7 天全功能免费试用。试用结束前如未取消，将自动续订为月度订阅（$0.99/月），可随时在系统设置中取消。

English: New users get a 7-day free trial with full access. Unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it auto-renews into a monthly subscription ($0.99/month). Manage or cancel anytime in system Settings.

我的数据存在哪里？/ Where is my data stored?

中文：本应用完全离线运行，所有训练记录仅保存在你的设备本地，我们不上传任何数据。

English: The app is fully offline. All training records stay on your device; we never upload any data.

管理订阅 / Manage subscription

中文：前往 iPhone「设置」→ 顶部你的姓名 → 「订阅」即可管理或取消。

English: Go to iPhone Settings → your name at the top → Subscriptions to manage or cancel.
